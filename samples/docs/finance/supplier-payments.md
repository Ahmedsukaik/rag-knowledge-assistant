# Supplier Payment Runs (Finance only)

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. This is not a real procedure.

## Payment run schedule

Supplier payment runs take place every Tuesday and Thursday. Invoices must be approved in the finance system by 12:00 on the working day before a run.

## Approval limits for supplier payments

- Up to £5,000: a finance officer.
- From £5,000 to £25,000: the finance manager.
- Above £25,000: the finance director and one other director.

## New suppliers and bank details

The bank details of a new supplier are confirmed by a call-back to a known phone number before the first payment. Changes to bank details received by email are never applied without this call-back.

## Urgent payments

Urgent same-day payments are allowed only for payroll errors and legal deadlines, with approval from the finance director.
