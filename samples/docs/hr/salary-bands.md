# Salary Bands 2027 (HR only)

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. All figures are invented.

## How bands work

Every role is mapped to one of five salary bands. Pay within a band depends on experience and performance. Moving to a higher band requires a promotion approved by the HR director.

## Bands

- Band A, entry level: £24,000 to £29,000
- Band B, specialist: £29,000 to £38,000
- Band C, senior specialist: £38,000 to £52,000
- Band D, manager: £52,000 to £70,000
- Band E, director: £70,000 to £95,000

## Annual pay review

Pay is reviewed every April. The 2027 pay review budget is 3.5% of the total salary bill. Managers send their recommendations to HR by 15 February.

## Confidentiality

This document is restricted to the HR team. Individual salaries are never shared outside HR and payroll.
