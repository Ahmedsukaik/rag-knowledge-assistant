# Onboarding Guide for New Starters

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. This is not a real guide.

## Your first day

Arrive at 9:30 at the reception on the second floor. Your manager meets you there. Bring photo ID so that reception can issue your building pass.

## Your first week

- Collect your laptop from the IT service desk and set up multi-factor authentication.
- Complete the mandatory training in the learning portal: security awareness, data protection, and health and safety.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who answers day-to-day questions during your first three months.

## Probation

The probation period is 6 months. Your manager holds review meetings with you after 3 months and after 6 months.

## Benefits

You are enrolled in the company pension scheme after one month of service. Northwind Demo Ltd contributes 5% of your base salary and you contribute 4%. Private health insurance starts when you pass probation.

## Who to ask

- Payroll and contracts: the HR team, hr@northwind-demo.example
- Laptops, accounts and access: the IT service desk, extension 4444
- Expense claims: your line manager first, then the finance team
