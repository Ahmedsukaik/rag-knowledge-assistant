# Leave Policy

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. This is not a real policy.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Book leave in the HR portal. For absences longer than three days, book at least two weeks in advance. Your line manager approves or declines the request within five working days.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to 5 unused days of annual leave into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, or they are lost. In exceptional cases, such as long-term sick leave, HR can approve a longer carry-over period.

## Sick leave

Tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. For absences longer than seven calendar days, a doctor's note is required.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 26 weeks of parental leave, of which the first 16 weeks are at full pay. Partners receive 4 weeks of parental leave at full pay, to be taken within the first year. Adoption leave follows the same rules.

## Sabbaticals

After three years of service you may request an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. Send the request to your line manager and HR at least three months before the start date.

## Public holidays

The office is closed on public holidays. If you are asked to work on a public holiday, you receive a day off in lieu.
