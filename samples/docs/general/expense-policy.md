# Expense Policy

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. This is not a real policy.

## What you can claim

You can claim reasonable costs of travel, accommodation and meals while working away from your usual office. Client entertainment must be agreed with your manager in advance.

## Limits

- Hotels: up to £150 per night in London and £110 per night elsewhere.
- Meals while travelling: up to £35 per day.
- Rail travel: standard class. First class only with director approval.
- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours.

## Approval

Every expense claim needs approval from your line manager. Claims above £500 also need approval from the finance team. No claim is approved automatically.

## How to submit a claim

Submit claims in the expenses app within 30 days of the expense, with a photo of each receipt. Claims older than 90 days are not paid.

## Reimbursement

Approved claims are paid back with the next monthly payroll. Claims approved after the 20th of the month are paid the following month.

## Company cards

Company credit cards are issued to staff who travel more than twice a month. Card statements must be matched to receipts in the expenses app by the 5th of each month.
