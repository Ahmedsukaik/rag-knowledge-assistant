# IT Security Policy

Demo document for Northwind Demo Ltd, a fictional company. This is not a real policy.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager for every work account. Never reuse a work password on another website.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email, the HR portal, the expenses app and remote access. Use the authenticator app. Codes sent by text message are not accepted.

## Laptops and phones

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes without use. Only install software from the self-service catalogue. Personal phones may access work email only through the managed mail app.

## Reporting a security incident

Report lost or stolen devices, suspicious emails and suspected breaches to the IT service desk straight away, at security@northwind-demo.example or extension 4444. Do not investigate on your own. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported within one hour.

## Working remotely

Use the company VPN on public Wi-Fi. Do not work on confidential documents in public places where others can read your screen.

## Data classification

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted documents may only be stored in the system that owns them, for example the HR system.
